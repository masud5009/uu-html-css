About the league

One home for the whole league

The Uttara University Premier League (UUPL) is the annual inter-department tennis-ball cricket tournament of Uttara University — fixtures, results, standings and registration, all in one place.

  • Every department, one field

    All fourteen departments from every school fight for a single trophy.
  • Fixtures & results in real time

    Schedules, live scores and the points table after every ball.
  • Campus rules, campus spirit

    From the window law to one-tip catches, the game stays true to its home ground.
