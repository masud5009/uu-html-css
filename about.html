About the UUPL Project

One home for the whole league

The Uttara University Premier League (UUPL) is an inter-department cricket tournament, and this site is where it lives: fixtures, results, standings, the rulebook and team registration, all in one place.

It is built with semantic HTML5, Bootstrap 5 for layout and components, and a little jQuery for the interactive parts: the headline counters, the photo viewer and form validation.
