Official League Rules

1. Match Format & Overs

Each innings is 8 overs played with a red-taped tennis ball. 7 active players per side with unlimited rolling substitutions.

2. The Window & Balcony Law

Hitting the ball directly into a neighbor's window, balcony, or rooftop water tank is an immediate OUT. The batter must retrieve the ball.

3. One-Tip, One-Hand Catch

If the ball rebounds once off an alley wall, shutter, or boundary grill, a clean one-handed catch is declared OUT by the umpire.

4. Bowling & Pace Limit

Maximum 2 overs per bowler with a strict 5-step short run-up. High bouncers above chest height are called no-ball immediately.

5. Lost Ball & Car Hazard

If the ball gets stuck under parked cars or inside drains, play pauses. Both teams must search. If unrecoverable, batting side provides the new tape ball.

6. Last Man Standing

When 6 wickets fall, the lone batter may bat alone. Runs can only be scored in pairs (2s, 4s, 6s) or by completing a full round-trip sprint.

7. Traffic & Street Pause

When a rickshaw, bicycle, or neighborhood elder enters the alley, dead-ball is signaled immediately. Zero arguments allowed.

8. Points & Standings

2 points awarded for a win, 0 for a loss. Tied matches are decided by a 1-over Super Over. Net Run Rate (NRR) determines standings ties.
