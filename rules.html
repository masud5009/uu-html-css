Official League Rules

1. Match Format & Overs

Each innings is 8 overs played with a red-taped tennis ball. 7 active players per department with unlimited rolling substitutions.

2. Campus Boundary & Window Law

Hitting the ball directly into campus academic windows, lab glass, or department balconies is an immediate OUT. The batter must retrieve the ball.

3. One-Tip, One-Hand Catch

If the ball rebounds once off a campus boundary wall, corridor pillar, or grill, a clean one-handed catch is declared OUT by the umpire.

4. Bowling & Pace Limit

Maximum 2 overs per bowler with a strict 5-step short run-up. High bouncers above chest height are called no-ball immediately.

5. Lost Ball Protocol

If the ball gets stuck under campus vehicles or into drains, play pauses. Both departments must search. If unrecoverable, batting side provides the new tape ball.

6. Last Man Standing

When 6 wickets fall, the lone batter may bat alone. Runs can only be scored in pairs (2s, 4s, 6s) or by completing a full round-trip sprint.

7. Faculty & Pedestrian Pause

When university faculty, campus visitors, or campus vehicles cross the field pathway, dead-ball is signaled immediately. Zero arguments allowed.

8. Points & Standings

2 points awarded for a win, 0 for a loss. Tied matches are decided by a 1-over Super Over. Net Run Rate (NRR) determines department standings ties.
