League rules

Match format

Each innings is 8 overs with a tennis ball. Each team has 11 players.

Bowling

A bowler can bowl a maximum of 2 overs.

Scoring

A win gives 2 points. A tie or no result gives 1 point each.

Fair play

Umpire decisions are final. Players must respect the opposition and the ground.
