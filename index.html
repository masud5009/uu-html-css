Cricket, played on campus terms

Match Format & Overs

Each innings is 8 overs played with a red-taped tennis ball. 7 active players per department with unlimited rolling substitutions.

Campus Boundary & Window Law

Hit the ball straight into an academic window, lab glass or balcony and you are out. Then you go and fetch it.

One-Tip, One-Hand Catch

Off a boundary wall, corridor pillar or grill, one bounce, one hand: a clean catch is out.

Bowling & Pace Limit

Maximum 2 overs per bowler with a strict 5-step short run-up. High bouncers above chest height are called no-ball immediately.

Lost Ball Protocol

Ball stuck under campus vehicles or into a drain pauses play: both departments search, and if unrecoverable the batting side provides the new tape ball.

Last Man Standing

When 6 wickets fall, the lone batter may bat alone. Runs can only be scored in pairs (2s, 4s, 6s) or by completing a full round-trip sprint.

Faculty & Pedestrian Pause

When faculty, visitors or vehicles cross the field, it is dead ball at once. Zero arguments.

Points & Standings

2 points for a win, 0 for a loss. Tied matches are decided by a 1-over Super Over, with Net Run Rate breaking standings ties.

Umpire's Call Is Final

Every out, no-ball and boundary call belongs to the umpires on duty. Arguing a decision only wastes balls — respect the whistle and play on.

About the league

One home for the whole league

The Uttara University Premier League (UUPL) is the annual inter-department tennis-ball cricket tournament of Uttara University — fixtures, results, standings and registration, all in one place.

  • Every department, one field

    All fourteen departments from every school fight for a single trophy.
  • Fixtures & results in real time

    Schedules, live scores and the points table after every ball.
  • Campus rules, campus spirit

    From the window law to one-tip catches, the game stays true to its home ground.
